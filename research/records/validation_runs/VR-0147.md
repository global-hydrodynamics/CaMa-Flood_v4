## Scope and interpretation

Added p01-f01d, `raster_river_map-subbasin_color`, to the current f01 Figure.
The wrapper has editable subcol file/palette/white-mix settings, a 2x2 default
grid and the existing FIG_WIDTH/FIG_HEIGHT/DPI/GAP/MARGIN controls. f01a-c
scientific scripts are byte-identical to the starting commit; grid allocation
changes their physical panel sizes, not their calculations or styling.

The new reader consumes exactly one little-endian int32 NX*NY record with x
varying fastest, matching Fortran (NX,NY) storage. Missing -9999 and unassigned
zero values are masked; unexpected indices outside 1-10 are rejected. The
tracked 38,400-byte sample has 80x120 cells, 5,292 assigned cells, 4,308 masked
cells and display indices 2-6. Its paired subbsn map has 64 positive segment
IDs; zero denotes unassigned network cells. Display indices are not unique IDs.

The palette is identical to f01c for every index (tab10 plus 60% opaque white).
The colorbar includes only present indices. The later maintainer refinement
retains subcol as the main fill and adds:

- Black 0.35-point shared cell edges between adjacent, positive, different
  original basin IDs from `netcdf/basin.nc`. No display-index contours or
  interpolation are used. The sample produces 2,246 boundary-edge segments.
- Neutral gray `#b3b3b3` on 6,147 cells missing/non-finite in either component
  of `netcdf/nextxy_noedge.nc`. Finite -9/-10 terminal codes remain valid.
  This is a no-edge network-validity mask, not a land/sea mask. It takes
  precedence over the main fill's missing/unassigned white background.

The preparation references are the actual s06 wrappers, analysis_subbasin.F90
and script_params/README.md. The 30,000 km² value is an upstream-area-increment
threshold: a qualifying current cell exceeds the threshold, and its downstream
area increase exceeds the threshold. It does not impose a universal minimum
segment size. The no-edge preparation is documented from region_cut_domain.F90.
No map product is regenerated, converted or changed by plotting.
