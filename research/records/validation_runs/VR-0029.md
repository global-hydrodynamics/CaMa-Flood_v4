## Interpretation and limits

Byte identity demonstrates mechanical migration compatibility only for the tested GNU Fortran build, `BIFLAYER=5`, and Mekong inputs. It is not independent scientific validation, does not cover MPI processing, and does not establish equivalence for other compilers or map datasets. No CaMa-Flood simulation was run.
