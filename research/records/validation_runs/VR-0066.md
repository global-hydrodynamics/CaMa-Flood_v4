## Interpretation and limits

The tests and source review support the documented distinction between the single-area allocation contract and source-level multi-area aggregation capability. They do not establish full-dataset multi-area numerical correctness, performance, or scientific suitability. The maintainer-operated large-data test remains pending; no regionalization, allocation, input-matrix, or permanent-water program was run against full package map data during this validation.

The v5 allocation output registry entries describe the observed fixed-width text schemas. Presence in the working Mekong sample and parity fixtures is not a scientific assessment of individual gauge or dam locations. `workflow_stage` was preserved as manually maintained.
