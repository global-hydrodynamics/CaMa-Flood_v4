## Results

The forced allocation build compiled all three executables successfully with GNU Fortran 14.2.0. The full map-tools test discovery ran 56 tests: 55 passed and one existing runoff-climatology-dependent test was skipped because `CMF_RUNOFF_CLIMATOLOGY` was not set. The allocation regression passed against the saved v4 snapshots for all six persistent output files, including flow single/multi modes, dam river/small/error classifications, and synthetic level-gauge Type 1/2/3 cases. Wrapper execution passed in temporary map-shaped layouts. Shell syntax checks passed. The registry validator accepted 84 entries, the Mekong sample validator passed six tests, the research validator accepted all record IDs/schemas, and the diff whitespace check passed. All five approved allocation CSVs were tracked, not ignored, retained the `text` attribute, and had no worktree content changes. GNU Fortran also emitted possible REAL(8)-to-REAL(4) conversion and optimizer `kx`/`ky` maybe-uninitialized warnings for the migrated copies; the same warnings occur when compiling the preserved v4 sources with the same flags. Source control flow and regression were inspected, and these legacy warnings were left unchanged rather than altering the approved algorithms.

- Result: **pass**
- Numerical comparison: floating output fields compared within half of the least-significant printed unit; identifiers, categories, sentinels, output order, and schema compared exactly.
- Sample safety: the regression hashes relevant committed map inputs before and after temporary test runs and confirms they are unchanged.

## Interpretation and limitations

These checks establish software buildability and behavior parity for selected Mekong and synthetic cases in this compiler environment. They do not independently validate the locations or scientific accuracy of individual gauge/reservoir allocations, nor the rationale for legacy thresholds. `KG-0012` remains an important open scientific question. `KG-0013` retains row-level provenance questions for the additional GRanD/Mekong records. Multi-area allocation was outside Phase B; this validation did not assess multi-area aggregation workflows, which are documented separately after source review in `KG-0014`. The historical v4 MLIT filename origin remains undocumented even though the v5 input is resolved (`KG-0015`).
