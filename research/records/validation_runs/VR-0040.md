## Interpretation and limits

The sample validator encodes an older regionalization-stage inventory and expects the legacy `diminfo_test-1deg.txt` descriptor. The current workspace has generated parameter/bifurcation products and the v5 `inpdim_` naming, while the old descriptor is absent. This explains the observed failures but does not establish which generated files are intended to remain in the official sample or whether the old descriptor's removal is permanent. No files were modified by these checks. The maintainer's manual refresh is not independently validated, and this run is not a scientific validation.
