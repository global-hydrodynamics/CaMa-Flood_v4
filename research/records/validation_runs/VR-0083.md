The generator used the existing `map/tools/code/param_inpmat_generate`. It wrote `inpdim_test-mek_1deg.txt` and `inpmat_test-mek_1deg.bin` into the Mekong map directory. The descriptor reports map dimensions 80 × 120, 10 floodplain layers, runoff dimensions 20 × 30, `INPN=4`, and bounds 90–110°E / 5–35°N. The matrix is 460,800 bytes: 12 direct-access 32-bit records in x-index, y-index, and overlap-area groups. The x/y indices fall within the configured 20 × 30 grid; overlap values are finite and nonnegative.

The model ran from 2000-01-01 00:00 through 2001-01-01 00:00 in non-MPI mode with adaptive timestep enabled, bifurcation enabled, reservoir operation disabled, zero initial storage, and `rivwth_merit.bin`. The log reports the expected 80 × 120 and 20 × 30 × 4 dimensions, `LPTHOUT=T`, matrix initialization, 366 daily forcing reads from `Roff____20000101.one` through `Roff____20001231.one`, and 8,784 model steps. The bifurcation table was read by the model.

The four gridded outputs (`outflw2000.bin`, `storge2000.bin`, `sfcelv2000.bin`, and `flddph2000.bin`) each contain 366 records of 80 × 120 float32 values and are 14,054,400 bytes. `pthflw2000.pth`, `restart2001010100.bin`, and `restart2001010100.bin.pth` are nonempty. All scanned float values are finite. Gridded outputs include finite `1e20` fill values for missing cells. Negative `outflw` and `pthflw` values occur; source routing permits reverse flow, so their sign alone is not treated as a failure. Existing map and runoff products were not modified; the generated input matrix pair is retained with the map sample.

The run products and namelist are under the Git-ignored `out/mek01-default_run/`. Pre-existing uncommitted moves of copied legacy scripts under `gosh_sample/legacy/` were left untouched and are not included in the Phase 1 changes.

## Limitations

This run confirms that this build can read the sample map, matrix, and daily forcing and complete the configured simulation. It does not establish scientific validity, hydrologic plausibility, independent correctness of the input data, or numerical equivalence with v4. Optional MPI, NetCDF, restart continuation, reservoir operation, and other specialized schemes were not tested.
