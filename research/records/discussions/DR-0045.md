## Question or trigger

The Phase C assignment requested a source-based review of ERA5-Land retrieval and conversion, readability updates, lightweight synthetic tests, documentation, and removal of raw downloaded source data from the provisional product registry. Scientific timestamp and accumulation interpretation were explicitly protected from unapproved changes.

## Summary of findings and decisions

The registry's raw-source row was removed; the source data and its provenance remain documented in the ERA5-Land guide. Source inspection confirms that the downloader requests `reanalysis-era5-land` runoff at times beginning 00:00 UTC. Both converters assume accumulated metres, difference within UTC calendar-date groups, retain the first record by default, convert to millimetres, and apply the current negative-difference fallback and clipping. The binary converter's current CTL count is the number of output timestamps.

The tracked raw and converted Japan NetCDFs each have 31 daily timestamps. The tracked binary CTL/report declares 62 daily records, and ignored local daily files each contain two 321×221 float32 records. These do not match the tracked source and current 24-hour wrapper contract; the historical settings are unavailable. The evidence does not establish whether old settings or independent modification produced the mismatch.

The ERA5-Land documentation says a CDS 00:00 UTC accumulation represents the preceding 24 hours. The current code's calendar grouping and first-record behavior may not align with the intended simulation-day interpretation. KG-0019 remains open and no timestamp, accumulation, negative, missing-value, or output-unit behavior was changed.
