## Question and context

Phase A had found `GRDC_allocated.csv` and `GRanD_allocated.csv` in the package but could not establish their detailed preprocessing method or whether the processed files could be redistributed. The task also asked why these high-resolution allocations are suitable inputs to CaMa-Flood's allocation programs.

## Evidence examined

- AllocRiverGauge v1.2 manual sections on its two-step method, GRDC preparation, and GRanD preparation; the earlier v1.1 copy in the v4.30 package was also checked and describes the same two-stage relationship.
- The legacy CaMa-Flood input wrappers and allocation-program contracts summarized in `docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md`.
- Maintainer clarification about the first-stage correction of reported drainage areas and MERIT Hydro/real-world area discrepancies, expected consistency with CaMa-Flood unit catchments, and the limited redistribution permission.

## Alternatives discussed

The earlier plan considered keeping the real tables external or using geographically subsetted derivatives. The updated proposal is to track the complete processed tables because they are small and redistribution of these exact files is confirmed. No proposal extends permission to source databases, alternate derivatives, or MLIT files.

## Proposal (not yet a decision)

Track the complete `GRDC_allocated.csv` and `GRanD_allocated.csv` in Git, retain source attribution, and do not make geographically filtered copies without a concrete technical requirement. This repository data-management proposal awaits maintainer approval before data or ignore rules change.

## Maintainer decision and rationale

The maintainer confirms that the two named processed CSVs contain metadata only and may be redistributed. During AllocRiverGauge's first-stage allocation, reported drainage-area errors and discrepancies between MERIT Hydro and real-world areas were addressed. Since CaMa-Flood networks derive from MERIT Hydro, the maintainer expects the allocated locations and areas to be internally consistent when mapped to CaMa-Flood unit catchments. This is a rationale and expectation, not evidence that every row is accurate.

## Implementation instruction, if any

Update the Phase A migration plan and research knowledge-gap records only. Do not migrate tools, add CSVs, change `.gitignore`, alter algorithms, or commit data in this task.

## Outcome or unresolved questions

The manual documents semi-automated high-resolution allocation, manual/visual QA/QC, attribute correction, and the finalized GRDC/GRanD tables as inputs to CaMa-Flood's second step. Maintainer knowledge supplies the additional correction rationale and the consistency expectation. The proposal to track the complete two tables still needs approval. MLIT provenance/permission, the relation of `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv` to the documented final table, and scientific validation of individual allocations remain unresolved.
