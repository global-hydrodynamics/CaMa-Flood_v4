## Context and approved scope

The task authorized a v5 migration of legacy `map/src/src_param/calc_prmwat.F90` into the shared map-tools architecture. It explicitly keeps `map/src/`, `src/`, and `gosh/` unchanged. The new program and `script_params/s05-permanent_water.sh` operate independently of s01–s04, use an explicit `HIRES_TAG` default, and support the existing non-tiled high-resolution area list.

## Maintainer-confirmed scientific and product semantics

- Positive high-resolution `rivwth` values represent channel width at river-centerline pixels; `rivwth == -1` represents water-surface pixels outside the centerline.
- `prmwat` is an estimate of permanent-water surface area aggregated per unit catchment. It supports post-processing that excludes pre-existing permanent water when estimating newly inundated area; the present simulation core does not read it.
- The legacy fallback estimates a pixel area from a rectangle ten times larger in each direction and divides by 100. The maintainer identified this as a way to reduce round-off fluctuations at very fine resolutions. Numerical accuracy was not independently quantified in this migration.
- The legacy `flddif <= 5 m` rule is retained. The task states that the origin of the 5 m threshold has not been verified.

## Evidence and implementation

The legacy source uses `(rivwth > 0 OR rivwth == -1) AND flddif <= 5`, accumulates pixel areas converted from km2 to m2, and caps each value at `ctmare`. The new source preserves those rules and the direct-record float32 output contract while adding input/layout, index, area, and I/O checks. A diagnostic warning preserves the legacy inclusion of `flddif=-9999`; no new filter was introduced. See `map/tools/code/param_water_calc_prmwat.F90`, `map/tools/script_params/s05-permanent_water.sh`, and `map/map_variables.csv`.

## Alternatives and boundaries

The task rejected silently changing scientific classification to exclude missing `flddif`, or replacing the fallback formula with a different area calculation. Tiled high-resolution input is not added because the inspected workflow and requested compatibility target are the non-tiled `location.txt` layout. No sample binary was regenerated in place.

## Outcome and open questions

The Mekong output matched legacy bytes with the supplied `grdare` data; a small synthetic fallback case also matched bytes. This is compatibility evidence, not independent scientific validation. Two nonblocking scientific questions remain recorded in KG-0009 and KG-0010: the historical rationale for 5 m and whether `flddif=-9999` should remain eligible in a future explicitly approved behavior change.
