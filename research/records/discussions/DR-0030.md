## Question and context

The research framework described recording knowledge gaps after discovery, but did not provide an explicit routine for discovering them at development milestones. The task requested a lightweight, evidence-traceable recovery method and proactive review integrated into completion reports.

## Evidence examined

- Current rules in `AGENTS.md` and `docs/ai_development_workflow.md`.
- Research index, study protocol, knowledge-gap records KG-0001 through KG-0005, and existing research templates.
- The task's explicit maintainer requirements; no private conversation archive was consulted.

## Alternatives discussed

No alternatives were supplied. The approved direction extends existing records and templates instead of adding a separate database or mandatory review after every commit.

## Maintainer decision and rationale

The maintainer approved a structured but short review at completion of substantial work. It should look for unclear scientific assumptions or thresholds, undocumented data contracts/dependencies, unreconstructable decisions, cross-source inconsistencies, and unsupported AI assumptions. Questions must be recorded before resolution, unresolved material questions returned together for maintainer review, and resolution provenance linked. Noncritical answers do not block unrelated work. A completed review with no material new gaps is a valid outcome; records must not be fabricated to populate the framework.

## Implementation instruction, if any

Update `AGENTS.md`, AI workflow guidance, research README, knowledge-recovery protocol and relevant templates. Preserve the current record schemas and existing history unless a schema change is necessary. Validate research tables and links. No source-code or map-data changes are in scope.

## Outcome or unresolved questions

The knowledge-recovery protocol and milestone checklist were added. A limited audit linked existing KG records without upgrading unverified historical hypotheses. The current task's review found no additional scientific gap to assert. Future systematic v4-baseline comparisons listed in the protocol remain unevaluated.
