# DR-0040: Distributed map layers and allocation-only high-resolution maps

- Discussion ID: `DR-0040`

- Maintainer review: pending

## Question and context

The AllocRiverGauge manual describes a global 30-arcsec map and Japan 5-arcsec map for its first allocation stage. The v4.30 CaMa-Flood distribution instead has global 1-minute and Japan 15-second high-resolution map directories. Earlier records treated the relationship as unresolved.

## Evidence examined

- Read-only inventory of `map/glb_15min/1min/location.txt` in the v4.30 distribution: one area, 21600 x 10800 cells, global extent, 1 arcmin cell size.
- Read-only inventory of `map/tej_01min/15sec/location.txt`: one area, 6000 x 5280 cells, extent 123–148 E and 24–46 N, 15 arcsec cell size. The directory contains 22 files: 11 binary layers, 10 CTLs, and `location.txt`; total binary size is 1,108,800,000 bytes.
- AllocRiverGauge v1.2 manual, which documents its first-stage 30-arcsec global and 5-arcsec Japan high-resolution allocation maps.

## Maintainer decision and rationale

The 30-second global and 5-second Japan grids are allocation-specific maps used in the AllocRiverGauge workflow. They are distinct from the global 1-minute and Japan 15-second map layers distributed with CaMa-Flood. The packaged 1-minute and 15-second datasets are the CaMa-Flood map inputs intended for the second-stage allocation workflow. This resolves the apparent resolution mismatch; it does not assert a derivation relationship between the grids or prove individual allocation accuracy.

The maintainer also clarified that `dam_param.csv` belongs to the later `src_dam` workflow and intentionally removed it from the current `map_variables.csv` review. Do not restore it as an allocation-stage product.

## Outcome and limits

The technical plan, allocation workflow README, and `KG-0014` now distinguish first-stage allocation maps from distributed CaMa-Flood high-resolution layers. Both inspected package `location.txt` files declare one area. Multi-area processing validation remains a separate task for a package dataset that actually declares multiple areas. No map data or algorithms were changed, and no scientific validation of allocation results is claimed.
