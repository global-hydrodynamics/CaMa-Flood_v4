## Maintainer decision and rationale

The maintainer directed that declarations be grouped by scientific or computational purpose; closely related variables (such as dimension and coordinate pairs) remain together; variables with different meanings receive separate declarations even when that increases line count; concise inline comments describe roles, physical units, and classifications where known; and short comments, blank lines, and reasonable alignment make groups easier to scan. The declaration area should function as a human-readable inventory.

## Implementation instruction, if any

Apply the convention to all existing Fortran files under `map/tools/code/`. Preserve completed readability work and all unrelated worktree edits. Do not change scientific calculations, numerical behavior, public interfaces, or established I/O. Update `AGENTS.md` and `docs/ai_development_workflow.md`, and continue validation.
