# KG-0035: Outlet coordinates versus high-resolution catchment assignment

- Gap ID: KG-0035
- Linked event ID(s): EV-0428, EV-0429
- Category: map-product correspondence / coordinate resolution
- Evidence basis: source inspection and direct comparison of tracked map products
- Status: open

## What remains unknown?

For 143 of the 6,507 coarse-map outlet positions that fall inside the Mekong 1-minute map domain, the containing 1-minute pixel's `catmxy` assignment is not the same coarse cell as the outlet's `lonlat` cell. The intended interpretation and provenance of these cases are not established. This record does not assume that either map product is wrong.

## How was it identified?

While preparing Tutorial 02 panels that overlay coarse unit-catchment outlet coordinates on high-resolution `catmxy` catchment assignments, the selected delta outlets were checked against the high-resolution map. All 21 outlets displayed in the chosen delta extent map back to their expected coarse cells, but a whole-domain comparison found the 143 mismatches. The distinction matters because a map visualization should not imply universal one-to-one agreement when the stored products do not demonstrate it.

## Verified facts

- Coarse outlet coordinates are stored as `outlon` and `outlat` in `map/mekong_15min/netcdf/lonlat.nc`.
- High-resolution pixel-to-coarse-cell assignments are stored in the two records of `map/mekong_15min/1min/1min.catmxy.bin`; the grid geometry is in `map/mekong_15min/1min/location.txt`.
- Using the documented bounds/resolution and nearest pixel index rule, 6,507 positive-uparea outlet positions fall inside the 1-minute domain; 143 corresponding pixels have a different `(catmxx,catmyy)` assignment.
- The 21 outlet points included in the Tutorial 02 delta panel map to their selected coarse cells.
- The source materials inspected for this task do not explain the whole-domain mismatches. No map data were changed.

## How was the knowledge formalized?

The verified counts and limitation are documented in `tutorial/tutorial02-mekong_netcdf_4exp/tutorial_design.md`, Section 4.6 and Appendix A.2. The visualization script validates selected locations and reports their assignments; it does not attempt to reconcile mismatched map products.

## Verification and limits

The 143/6,507 count was independently recomputed from the two map products. The comparison only establishes a coordinate-to-pixel assignment difference under the documented grid geometry. It does not identify which product or spatial scale should govern a future visualization, nor does it establish an error in either product.

## Why the gap matters

Future visualizations may combine outlet locations and high-resolution catchment boundaries. Until the product relationship is understood, figures should report the observed limitation and avoid presenting all outlet-to-catchment correspondences as exact.
