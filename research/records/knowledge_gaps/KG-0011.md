# KG-0011: Exact-5 m boundary in the permanent-water elevation filter

- Gap ID: `KG-0011`
- Linked event IDs: `EV-0175`, `EV-0176`, `EV-0177`
- Category: scientific threshold / comparator semantics
- Evidence basis: maintainer clarification compared with v4 and v5 source
- Status: open

## What remains unknown?

Should high-resolution permanent-water pixels with `flddif == 5 m` contribute to `prmwat`? The maintainer described the intended condition as strict `flddif < 5 m`; both the inspected legacy v4 source and the migrated v5 implementation use `flddif <= 5 m` and include equality.

## How was it discovered?

The maintainer clarified that the elevation threshold excludes isolated permanent lakes above CaMa-Flood's modeled inundation domain. Comparing the stated strict criterion with `map/src/src_param/calc_prmwat.F90` and `map/tools/code/param_water_calc_prmwat.F90` revealed the comparator mismatch. The documentation follow-up did not authorize changing established numerical behavior.

## Verified facts

- The legacy source uses `flddif0 <= 5`.
- The migrated source uses `flddif <= 5.0_real32`.
- The maintainer's conceptual criterion was stated as `flddif < 5 m`.
- The existing synthetic test verifies legacy inclusion at exactly 5 m; the Mekong sample comparison remains byte-identical.
- Changing `<=` to `<` changes results for selected pixels whose stored `flddif` is exactly 5 m. No affected-cell count has been computed for general datasets.

## How was the knowledge formalized?

The v5 source comments, shared and Mekong parameter READMEs, map metadata specification, registry generation note, and ADR-0004 now state the discrepancy. The comparator remains inclusive until the maintainer confirms whether strict behavior is intended.

## Resolution trace

- Resolution method: unresolved pending maintainer decision
- Evidence or maintainer explanation: maintainer clarification in DR-0033; direct comparison of v4/v5 source.
- Final interpretation and rationale: document the physical role of the filter, preserve `<=` for now, and do not treat the wording `<5` as authorization for an unreviewed scientific behavior change.
- Formalization locations: `map/tools/code/param_water_calc_prmwat.F90`; both parameter READMEs; `map/map_metadata_spec.md`; `map/map_variables.csv`; ADR-0004.
- Human review: pending

## Verification and limits

The regression comparison establishes that v5 matches existing v4 behavior on the Mekong sample. The exact-5 m synthetic case confirms inclusive legacy behavior. It does not establish the maintainer's preferred treatment of equality for all future calculations.

## Why the gap matters

The comparator affects permanent-water area for any selected pixel exactly at the threshold. A silent code change could alter downstream estimates; leaving a mismatch undocumented could also mislead future implementations.
