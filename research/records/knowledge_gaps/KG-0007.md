# KG-0007: Rationale for the fine-resolution pixel-area fallback scaling

- Gap ID: `KG-0007`
- Linked event IDs: `EV-0170`, `EV-0171`, `EV-0172`
- Category: numerical method / historical rationale
- Evidence basis: legacy source plus maintainer-confirmed rationale
- Status: partially_formalized

## What was unknown or assumed?

The legacy `rgetarea` fallback multiplies the pixel size by ten in both dimensions, then applies `0.01`; the reason for the scaling was not evident from code alone.

## How was it discovered?

The migration had to preserve the optional fallback when an area's `grdare.bin` is absent. Replacing the formula without knowing its purpose could change generated values, especially at very fine resolutions. The maintainer explained the intended numerical-stability rationale in the approved task specification.

## Verified facts

- Legacy code calls `rgetarea` with `10*csize` and latitude bounds offset by `5*csize`, then multiplies by `0.01*1.e-6` to obtain km2.
- The maintainer confirmed the 10-by-10 rectangle/divide-by-100 approach is intended to reduce floating-point round-off fluctuations at resolutions such as 3 arcseconds and 1 arcsecond.
- This task did not quantify the accuracy improvement against a higher-precision reference.

## How was the knowledge formalized?

Comments in `map/tools/code/param_water_calc_prmwat.F90` and the permanent-water sections in `map/map_metadata_spec.md` and `map/tools/script_params/README.md` preserve the method and state its validation limit. ADR-0004 prohibits substituting another formula in this migration.

## Resolution trace

- Resolution method: mixed (source analysis and maintainer knowledge)
- Evidence or maintainer explanation: DR-0032 and HI-0004; `rgetarea` call in `map/src/src_param/calc_prmwat.F90`
- Final interpretation and rationale: the scaling is an intentional numerical-stability technique; maintain it, but do not claim measured accuracy.
- Formalization locations: v5 source, map metadata specification, parameter-workflow README
- Human review: pending

## Verification and limits

A synthetic fixture with `grdare` absent produced byte-identical old and new outputs. This tests faithful implementation of the fallback, not the magnitude of its numerical-stability benefit.

## Why the gap matters

A future refactor or conversion tool could otherwise replace this less-obvious formula and silently alter area estimates.
