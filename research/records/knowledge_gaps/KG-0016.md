# KG-0016: Consolidating the two-stage allocation workflow

- Gap ID: `KG-0016`
- Linked event ID(s): `EV-0179`, `EV-0181`, `EV-0182`, `EV-0193`, `EV-0196`
- Category: knowledge consolidation / accessibility; data provenance
- Evidence basis: AllocRiverGauge manual, legacy source, and maintainer clarification
- Status: formalized

## What was unknown or assumed?

Phase A initially lacked an accessible account of how the GRDC/GRanD high-resolution allocation products related to CaMa-Flood's own allocation tools, and whether this implied record-level accuracy.

## How was it discovered?

Phase A found that the CaMa-Flood programs consumed `*_allocated.csv` files but did not perform their high-resolution preparation. This raised a workflow/provenance question. Inspection of the existing AllocRiverGauge manual showed that the two-stage method was already documented there. The subsequent work therefore primarily consolidated and linked existing knowledge, rather than recovering the basic two-stage method as new scientific knowledge.

## Verified facts

- AllocRiverGauge manual v1.2 and the v4.30 package's earlier v1.1 manual document Step 1 allocation and review on high-resolution MERIT Hydro/J-FlwDir, followed by Step 2 mapping to a coarse CaMa-Flood map.
- The manual describes semi-automated matching, inspection/QAQC, and manual correction or exclusion of suspicious station/reservoir information; it identifies the finalized GRDC/GRanD tables as Step 2 inputs.
- The maintainer additionally confirmed that first-stage preparation addressed errors in reported drainage areas and discrepancies between MERIT Hydro and real-world drainage areas.
- The maintainer's rationale is that CaMa-Flood river networks derive from MERIT Hydro, so the first-stage locations/areas are expected to be internally consistent with CaMa-Flood unit catchments.
- This expectation is a design rationale, not evidence that every allocation is accurate. Neither manual evidence nor the maintainer explanation independently validates any individual record.

## Knowledge classification

- **Knowledge consolidation:** the manual's two-stage description was already available and is now connected to CaMa-Flood's reader inputs, code, and migration workflow.
- **Knowledge accessibility improvement:** the migration plan and tool documentation link to the manual and explain the stage boundary for future developers and agents.
- **Maintainer-confirmed context:** the additional description of reported-area corrections and expected consistency was supplied by the maintainer, not independently inferred from the manual.
- **Not established:** row-level correction history, record-level accuracy, and scientific validation.

## How was the knowledge formalized?

`docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md` describes the two stages, distinguishes manual evidence from the maintainer's additional explanation, documents the v5 input contract, and warns against treating parity or output generation as scientific validation. The v5 wrapper guide links to the AllocRiverGauge manual.

## Resolution trace

- Resolution method: documentation/source consolidation plus maintainer clarification for additional context.
- Evidence: AllocRiverGauge manuals v1.2 (`doc/Manual_AllocateRiverGauges.docx`, updated 2024-11-22) and package v1.1 (2024-04-16); `research/records/discussions/DR-0034.md`; maintainer instruction recorded in `EV-0193`.
- Final interpretation: CaMa-Flood allocation is the second-stage mapping of pre-allocated high-resolution locations/areas to CaMa-Flood catchments. Preserve the consistency rationale as an expectation only.
- Formalization: `docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md`, `map/tools/script_params/README.md`, and this record.
- Human review: maintainer-supplied rationale; record-level accuracy was not reviewed.

## Phase B knowledge-gap review

The Phase B review treated the AllocRiverGauge manual as existing documented scientific knowledge connected and made easier to find, not as newly recovered methodology. The maintainer-supplied consistency rationale is recorded separately from the manual's methodology. The migration regression does not prove individual allocation accuracy.

## Verification and limits

The two-stage method and named final products were inspected in the manuals. Regression tests establish parity with v4 for the tested Mekong records only. No individual station or reservoir was independently checked against field observations, and no scientific validation is claimed.

## Why the gap mattered

Without this distinction, v5 might repeat the first-stage corrections, misstate the expected input dataset, or overclaim accuracy from matching legacy results. Consolidating the manual and source contract improves discoverability without claiming new scientific evidence.
