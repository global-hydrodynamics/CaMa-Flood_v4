# KG-0016: Scientific basis and accuracy limits of the two-stage allocation

- Gap ID: `KG-0016`
- Linked event ID(s): `EV-0179`, `EV-0181`, `EV-0182`
- Category: scientific rationale / data provenance
- Evidence basis: AllocRiverGauge manual and maintainer-confirmed rationale; individual accuracy not established
- Status: partially_formalized

## What was unknown or assumed?

Why are the GRDC and GRanD high-resolution allocated locations and drainage areas used as inputs to the CaMa-Flood allocation programs, and what does that imply about the accuracy of individual mappings?

## How was it discovered?

The Phase A inventory found that CaMa-Flood's programs consume `*_allocated.csv` files but do not perform their high-resolution preparation. This raised a provenance and scientific-contract question before migration: whether the CaMa-Flood programs were meant to correct the original reported station/dam positions themselves or map already-reviewed high-resolution positions. The question was recorded in the Phase A plan, then clarified by the maintainer during follow-up.

## Verified facts

- The AllocRiverGauge tool-repository v1.2 manual (updated 2024-11-22; package v1.1 copy dated 2024-04-16) documents two stages: allocation to high-resolution MERIT Hydro/J-FlwDir river maps, followed by re-allocation to coarse CaMa-Flood maps.
- For GRDC and GRanD, the manual describes semi-automated matching, inspection and manual/visual QA/QC, attribute changes where suspicious data are found, repeated allocation, and final allocated tables intended for Step 2.
- The manual cautions that reported attributes or the high-resolution map may be wrong and recommends careful review; small rivers unresolved in CaMa-Flood may not be allocatable.
- The maintainer confirms that first-stage processing addressed errors in reported drainage areas and discrepancies between MERIT Hydro and real-world drainage areas.
- The maintainer's rationale is that CaMa-Flood river networks derive from MERIT Hydro; therefore the pre-allocated location/area inputs are expected to be internally consistent with CaMa-Flood unit catchments.
- Neither the manual nor this rationale proves the correctness of every individual record or the scientific validity of a CaMa-Flood output.

## How was the knowledge formalized?

`docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md` now distinguishes the manual's documented method from the additional maintainer explanation, identifies CaMa-Flood allocation as the second stage, and warns that consistency is an expectation rather than accuracy evidence.

## Resolution trace

- Resolution method: mixed; manual evidence plus maintainer knowledge
- Evidence or maintainer explanation: AllocRiverGauge v1.2 `doc/Manual_AllocateRiverGauges.docx` (updated 2024-11-22), with the v4.30 package's earlier v1.1 copy checked; maintainer clarification summarized in `research/records/discussions/DR-0034.md` and indexed by `EV-0181`.
- Final interpretation and rationale: use the prepared MERIT Hydro allocation as the intended first-stage result and map it to CaMa-Flood unit catchments in the second stage. Preserve the consistency rationale, but do not infer record-level accuracy or claim scientific validation.
- Formalization location(s): `docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md`; this record.
- Human review: pending

## Verification and limits

The manual's two-step method and named final products were inspected directly. The maintainer's additional area-correction and consistency explanation is recorded as human-provided knowledge. This task did not independently reallocate the datasets or assess individual stations/reservoirs, compare the outputs with field evidence, or establish which specific correction was applied to each row.

## Why the gap matters

The distinction determines the intended input contract and prevents v5 migration from duplicating first-stage scientific corrections. It also prevents implementation parity from being misrepresented as independent scientific validation.
