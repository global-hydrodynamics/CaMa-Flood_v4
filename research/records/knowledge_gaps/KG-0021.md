# KG-0021: Redistribution permission for ELSE_GPCC-derived sample data

- Gap ID: `KG-0021`
- Linked event ID(s): `EV-0239`
- Category: data redistribution rights / sample publication
- Evidence basis: Maintainer identifies the internal ELSE_GPCC global product and its Kim et al. (2009) provenance; local metadata and task evidence do not establish redistribution permission
- Status: open; generated regional binaries remain local and Git-ignored

## What is unknown?

Whether the internal ELSE_GPCC/Roff source or a regional subset may be redistributed through the public CaMa-Flood GitHub repository has not been established. A scholarly citation alone does not establish the dataset license or public redistribution rights.

## Impact

The extraction script, helper, output CTL, registry metadata, and documentation can be reviewed and version-controlled independently. The generated 1980–2010 Mekong runoff binaries must remain untracked until the maintainer confirms an applicable redistribution permission or an alternative approved distribution route.

## Resolution path

Obtain and record authoritative licensing/redistribution terms for the source product, then have the maintainer decide whether to publish the regional subset. No inference from local file availability or the Kim et al. paper should be treated as permission.
