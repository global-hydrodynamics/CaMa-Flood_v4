# KG-0013: Provenance and redistribution scope of allocation tables

- Gap ID: KG-0013
- Linked event IDs: EV-0179, EV-0181, EV-0182, EV-0184, EV-0185, EV-0187, EV-0188
- Category: data provenance / redistribution
- Evidence basis: local package observation, AllocRiverGauge manual, maintainer confirmations and tracking instruction; lineage of one alternate GRanD list remains unclear
- Status: partially_formalized

## What remains unknown?

The maintainer has directed that all GRDC, GRanD, and MLIT list CSVs currently in `map/data/` be tracked under their existing filenames. Redistribution permission was separately confirmed for `GRDC_allocated.csv`, `GRanD_allocated.csv`, `MLIT_LevelGauge_allocated.csv`, and `MLIT_FlowGauge_alloc_final.csv`. The provenance, precise role, and applicable redistribution basis of `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv` remain undocumented. The instruction does not cover source workbooks or unrestricted source databases.

## How was it discovered?

The allocation migration review found processed allocation tables excluded by the broad `/map/data/*` ignore rule and a mismatch between the legacy level-gauge wrapper and the available MLIT filename. Maintainer clarifications established the processed-table permissions and canonical MLIT names. The later instruction broadened the Git tracking scope to every GRDC, GRanD, and MLIT list CSV currently in `map/data/`, making the alternate GRanD filename a tracking target even though its lineage is unresolved.

## Verified facts and maintainer-confirmed decisions

- `map/data/` currently contains five relevant list CSVs: `GRDC_allocated.csv`, `GRanD_allocated.csv`, `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv`, `MLIT_LevelGauge_allocated.csv`, and `MLIT_FlowGauge_alloc_final.csv`.
- The AllocRiverGauge manual identifies `GRDC_allocated.csv` and `GRanD_allocated.csv` as finalized high-resolution allocations used by the CaMa-Flood second-stage workflow.
- The maintainer confirms that redistribution is permitted for the named processed GRDC, GRanD, and two MLIT tables described above; this is not blanket permission for source databases.
- The maintainer directs Git tracking of all five current GRDC/GRanD/MLIT list CSVs in `map/data/`, under their existing filenames.
- `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv` has 7,325 data rows and contains MERIT allocation fields, but its relationship to `GRanD_allocated.csv` and its exact purpose have not been established. Its name alone does not prove that it is a Mekong-only subset.
- The two MLIT files are maintainer-curated and approved for redistribution under their current names. The legacy level-gauge wrapper still references a different, absent filename; the v5 migration should address that separately.
- Non-list source workbooks and original databases are outside the current tracking instruction.

## How has the knowledge been formalized?

`docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md` lists the five current tracking targets, the scoped provenance statements, and the remaining alternate-file question. `.gitignore` has narrow exceptions for these five paths; `.gitattributes` treats them as text and normalizes repository line endings. Research records DR-0034 through DR-0036 distinguish manual evidence, prior permission decisions, and the new tracking instruction.

## Resolution trace

- Resolution method: maintainer-confirmed decisions for the named processed tables and tracking scope; package/manual evidence for observed files and documented workflow; unresolved source analysis for the alternate GRanD file.
- Evidence: AllocRiverGauge tool-repository manual v1.2 and package copy v1.1; local `map/data/` inventory; maintainer clarifications in EV-0181, EV-0184, and EV-0187.
- Final interpretation: track all five currently present list CSVs under their existing names. Do not claim the tracking instruction covers source workbooks/databases or resolves the exact lineage and rights basis for `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv`.
- Formalization: `docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md`, `docs/dev_status.md`, `.gitignore`, `.gitattributes`, and this record.
- Human review: pending.

## Why the gap matters

The tracking instruction is operationally clear, while the alternate file's scientific role and lineage are not. Keeping those claims separate preserves the maintainer's requested repository contents without inventing provenance or extending permissions to unrelated source products.
