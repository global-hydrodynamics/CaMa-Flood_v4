## What remains unknown?

The maintainer clarified that `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv` combines the GRanD list with several recently constructed Mekong dams added by additional research at Yamazaki Laboratory, and confirmed the file is appropriate to track. The specific added rows, supporting sources, and research/review date are not documented in this record.

The maintainer directed that all GRDC, GRanD, and MLIT list CSVs currently in `map/data/` be tracked under their existing filenames. Redistribution permission was separately confirmed for `GRDC_allocated.csv`, `GRanD_allocated.csv`, `MLIT_LevelGauge_allocated.csv`, and `MLIT_FlowGauge_alloc_final.csv`. The instruction does not cover non-list source workbooks or unrestricted source databases.

## How was it discovered?

The allocation migration review found processed allocation tables excluded by the broad `/map/data/*` ignore rule and a mismatch between the legacy level-gauge wrapper and the available MLIT filename. Maintainer clarifications established the processed-table permissions and canonical MLIT names. The tracking instruction then covered all five current lists. The filename `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv` initially left its relationship to the base GRanD list uncertain; the maintainer later supplied the broad provenance and research contribution.

## Verified facts and maintainer-confirmed decisions

- `map/data/` contains five relevant list CSVs: `GRDC_allocated.csv`, `GRanD_allocated.csv`, `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv`, `MLIT_LevelGauge_allocated.csv`, and `MLIT_FlowGauge_alloc_final.csv`.
- The AllocRiverGauge manual identifies `GRDC_allocated.csv` and `GRanD_allocated.csv` as finalized high-resolution allocations used by the CaMa-Flood second-stage workflow.
- The maintainer confirms redistribution is permitted for the named processed GRDC/GRanD and MLIT tables; this is not blanket permission for source databases.
- The maintainer directs tracking all five current GRDC/GRanD/MLIT list CSVs under their existing filenames.
- The maintainer says `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv` contains the GRanD list plus several recently constructed Mekong dams added by Yamazaki Laboratory following additional research, and confirms the file is appropriate to track.
- The exact added records, underlying sources, and investigation date were not specified. The broad dataset role is now known; this row-level provenance remains open if needed for audit or citation.
- Non-list source workbooks and original databases are outside the tracking instruction.
