# KG-0013: Provenance and redistribution rights of allocation tables

- Gap ID: KG-0013
- Linked event IDs: EV-0179
- Category: data provenance / redistribution
- Evidence basis: local package observation and official source terms; exact derivative-data rights unresolved
- Status: open

## What remains unknown?

Which source snapshots, preprocessing steps, manual edits, and permissions produced the allocation CSVs in the v4.30 package, and do those permissions allow distribution of these particular processed tables or regional subsets through GitHub and CaMa-Flood packages?

## How was it discovered?

The allocation scripts consume transformed tables rather than the original source workbooks. The inspected repository contains no end-to-end preparation procedure or per-file license record. Official GRDC conditions prohibit redistribution of downloaded data to third parties/the public; GRanD materials carry non-commercial and redistribution restrictions. MLIT's official terms allow reuse with attribution but identify possible third-party rights. These general terms do not resolve the status of each local derived file.

## Verified facts

- GRDC_allocated.csv, GRanD_allocated.csv, MLIT_LevelGauge_allocated.csv, and MLIT_FlowGauge_alloc_final.csv exist in the inspected package but are ignored by the repository's /map/data/* rule.
- The package also contains candidate source workbooks for GRDC/MERIT, GRanD v1.3/MERIT, and HydroWeb/MERIT, but their presence alone does not prove the exact table-generation chain.
- The official GRDC portal says downloaded hydrological data may not be redistributed to third parties or the general public and may not be used commercially.
- GRanD v1.3 is a 7,320-record source product; the local GRanD_allocated.csv has 7,320 rows. This matching count does not prove exact derivation or permissions.
- Official MLIT terms call for source attribution and warn that some linked/content material may carry third-party rights.

## How was the knowledge formalized?

docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md records actual file availability, sizes, row counts, sources consulted, and a conservative recommendation: no real dataset or geographic subset enters Git/CI until exact rights and provenance are confirmed. Synthetic fixtures are proposed instead.

## Verification and limits

The current official pages and historical source documentation support conservative restrictions, but this review did not establish the license attached to each specific CSV or workbook and is not legal advice. Confirm the exact source/version and permission before any redistribution.

## Why the gap matters

Publishing restricted station or dam records in a public repository or package could violate source terms. It also affects whether realistic data can be used in public CI or shared as reproducibility fixtures.
