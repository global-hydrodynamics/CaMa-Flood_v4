## What was unknown or assumed?

Does the no-Restart initialization produce a useful initial transient in Mekong daily discharge, how long does any effect persist, and is one calendar year adequate as spin-up for the proposed 2001–2003 analysis?

## How was it discovered?

Source review found that `CMF_PROG_INIT` initializes prognostic storage arrays to zero, then `STORAGE_SEA_SURFACE` sets river-channel storage to respect downstream boundary water level. The draft described an initial-condition teaching objective but did not establish the resulting magnitude or duration. This matters because the tutorial must not present the intended spin-up effect as an observed result before running the model.

## Verified facts

- The start is without a Restart when `LRESTART=.FALSE.`.
- River-channel storage can be initialized nonzero after prognostic arrays are zeroed.
- Source inspection cannot show whether daily discharge has a visible transient or whether one year is sufficient.
- Interannual runoff changes can also explain differences between calendar years.

## How was the knowledge formalized?

The cold-start behavior, testable hypothesis, leap-day plotting proposal, and non-claims are described in `tutorial/tutorial02-mekong_netcdf_3exp/experiment_spec.md`.
