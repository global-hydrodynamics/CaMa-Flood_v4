# KG-0002: Detailed original global-map generation procedure is not public

- Linked event: `EV-0006`
- Category: scientific workflow / provenance
- Evidence basis: maintainer-confirmed provenance recorded in current technical documentation; public methods paper
- Status: partially formalized; detailed procedure remains unavailable

## Verified and confirmed facts

The maintained map inventory states that original global maps were created in Yamazaki Laboratory, and the FLOW methodology is described at overview level in Yamazaki et al. (2011). Detailed production procedures are not public in the repository. Regional cropping utilities in `map/src/src_region/` must not be misrepresented as original global-map generators.

## Formalization and limits

The provenance and limits are recorded in [`docs/map_data_inventory.md`](../../../docs/map_data_inventory.md) and [`map/map_metadata_spec.md`](../../../map/map_metadata_spec.md). This record does not reconstruct undocumented scientific algorithms or claim the paper contains the private implementation details.

## Why it matters

Future map regeneration and cross-version comparisons need explicit input provenance and generation parameters. Work can proceed on formats/readers and package-based tests, while exact recreation of original global maps cannot be claimed.
