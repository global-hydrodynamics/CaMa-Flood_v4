# KG-0028: Non-geographic Map coordinate handling remains unverified

- Gap ID: `KG-0028`
- Linked event ID(s): `EV-0295`; `EV-0297`; `EV-0301`; `EV-0302`; `EV-0303`
- Category: map coordinates / output metadata
- Evidence basis: source inspection; maintainer clarification; Mekong bounds check; no GPA vector-map fixture
- Status: open

## What remains unclear?

In the individual Binary/NetCDF map path, `READ_MAP_FILES` assigns `D1LON` and `D1LAT` only when the map bounds pass the geographic range predicate. The maintainer clarified that the predicate is intended to calculate cell centers for regular geographic grids and skip non-geographic maps, including the historically supported GPA vector-map path. The latitude limits were corrected from `[-180, 180]` to `[-90, 90]`; the historical reason for the old `bugfix_v396a` limits is unknown. GPA maps are not currently available for a compatibility test. If the predicate is false, `D1LON` and `D1LAT` remain allocated but unassigned, while NetCDF output and restart writers may consume them. Their intended handling for a non-geographic map therefore remains unresolved.

## Discovery trigger and why it matters

The whole-file review traced map coordinate initialization in `READ_MAP_FILES` to its use in `cmf_ctrl_output_mod.F90` and `cmf_ctrl_restart_mod.F90`. The original latitude bounds were too broad for geographic coordinates. The maintainer clarified the guard's intended role and approved the latitude correction. The remaining concern is how GPA vector maps should supply coordinates to output and restart workflows; no behavior for that path was changed.

## Verified facts

- `D1LON(NX)` and `D1LAT(NY)` are allocated in `CMF_RIVMAP_INIT` before either network reader is called.
- The individual-file path calculates cell-center coordinates only inside the predicate at `src_main/cmf_ctrl_maps_mod.F90` in `READ_MAP_FILES`.
- The current predicate accepts longitude bounds within `[-180, 360]` and latitude bounds within `[-90, 90]`. The longitude limits were retained as approved; only latitude limits changed.
- The Mekong sample has `NX=80`, `NY=120`, resolution `0.25` degrees, and bounds `90..110 E`, `5..35 N`. Both the previous and corrected predicates accept it, so its computed center-coordinate values are unchanged (longitude `90.125..109.875`, latitude `34.875..5.125`).
- The maintainer described the old `bugfix_v396a` history as unavailable; its original rationale has not been independently reconstructed.
- The legacy consolidated reader obtains `lat` and `lon` from `CRIVCLINC` instead.
- NetCDF model output and NetCDF restart code write `D1LON` / `D1LAT`.

## Maintainer decision needed

When GPA vector-map support is restored, what coordinate representation should be provided to workflows that write NetCDF output or restart files, given that the regular-grid predicate is false and `D1LON` / `D1LAT` are not assigned on that path? This matters for coordinate correctness and GPA compatibility. The current geographic-grid correction and ordinary Mekong workflow can proceed independently; no fallback or new error behavior was added.

## Formalization

The broad concern was discovered during the `cmf_ctrl_maps_mod.F90` review (EV-0295; VR-0094). The maintainer later approved the geographic latitude-bound correction (EV-0301). The source change and Mekong-range check are recorded at EV-0302 and EV-0303 / VR-0096. GPA vector-map behavior remains unverified and is retained as the open portion of this gap.
