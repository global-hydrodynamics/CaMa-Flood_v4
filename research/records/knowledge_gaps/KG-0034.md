# KG-0034: Scientific suitability of Tutorial 02 GRDC comparison sites

- Gap ID: KG-0034
- Linked event ID(s): EV-0385
- Category: experiment design / station selection
- Evidence basis: allocated station metadata; scientific suitability not yet reviewed
- Status: open

## What remains unknown?

Do the five candidate GRDC allocation points provide a scientifically useful set for Tutorial 02's upstream-to-downstream hydrograph and floodplain comparison? Their presence and allocated map cells are verified, but their suitability and independence as teaching locations have not been established.

## How was it identified?

The existing source/data review found five allocated Mekong station records and explicitly left their final selection open. The four-experiment revision retains this requirement. This matters because the chosen locations determine which spatial responses students see and may affect the interpretation of floodplain contrasts.

## Evidence and limits

Candidate IDs, names, cells, and mapped upstream areas are listed in `tutorial/tutorial02-mekong_netcdf_4exp/experiment_spec.md`, Section 4.2. The GRDC records are used only as model-location references; no observed discharge is included.

## Resolution needed

The researcher should review and select the sites before tutorial analysis scripts and reference figures are finalized. No site has been declared scientifically preferred in this record.
