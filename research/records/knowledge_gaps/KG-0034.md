# KG-0034: Scientific suitability of Tutorial 02 sampling locations

- Gap ID: KG-0034
- Linked event ID(s): EV-0385, EV-0388, EV-0389, EV-0390
- Category: experiment design / station selection
- Evidence basis: maintainer-selected sites, GRDC allocation records, MRC station metadata, Mekong 15-minute map topology, and completed simulation outputs
- Status: open

## What remains unknown?

The maintainer selected Vientiane, Ubon Ratchathani, Stung Treng, Prek Kdam, and Can Tho as output-sampling targets. Their geographic references, allocated/model cells, and map connectivity were verified, and the four experiments produced time series there. Remaining uncertainty concerns how well the 0.25-degree map cells represent the intended local river reaches, especially the Mun tributary, Tonle Sap reversal reach, and Hau/Bassac delta. The runs establish simulated behavior at those cells, not agreement with observed flows or the scientific adequacy of the cell-to-site correspondence.

## How was it identified?

The earlier specification left the station set unresolved. The maintainer's implementation instruction selected five specific scientific targets and required connectivity review rather than nearest-cell assignment. The mapped cells were then traced and analyzed with the existing map-analysis tool. This matters because the selected cells define the plotted comparison and can shape interpretation of bifurcation and reverse-flow behavior.

## Evidence and limits

The selected IDs, station coordinates, model indices/centers, and mapped drainage areas are listed in `tutorial/tutorial02-mekong_netcdf_4exp/experiment_spec.md`, Section 4.2. Four sites use `GRDC_alloc.txt` and `map/data/GRDC_allocated.csv`; Can Tho uses MRC Water Quality Monitoring station H039803 coordinates, distinct from model cell (64,100). The individual NetCDF downstream indices match the Binary reference at all five cells. The Prek Kdam downstream chain reaches the selected Can Tho cell, and bifurcation paths 337/338 begin at that Can Tho cell.

All four experiment scripts ran 2000–2003. Prek Kdam has negative `outflw` in the two Local Inertial cases and no negative values in the two Kinematic Wave cases. At Can Tho, Default and No Bifurcation differ on all 1,095 comparison days. These are model results only; no observed discharge is included, and the MRC Can Tho source is a water-quality station report rather than a discharge record.

## Resolution needed

The location choice itself is maintainer-directed and implemented. The maintainer should review whether the coarse model representation is adequate for the intended scientific teaching claims, particularly Tonle Sap flow reversal and delta bifurcation sensitivity. Keep these interpretations provisional until that review; the beginner-facing README remains deferred.
