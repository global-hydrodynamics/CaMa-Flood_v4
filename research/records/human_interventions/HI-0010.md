# HI-0010 — Maintainer explanation of high-resolution area choices

- Intervention ID: `HI-0010`
- Linked event ID(s): `EV-0201`
- Task ID: `DT-0028`
- Category: scientific rationale and map-processing data contract
- Maintainer review: pending

## Trigger

During documentation of the v5 allocation migration, source inspection showed that allocation accepts exactly one high-resolution area. That behavior was already approved, but the scientific rationale and distinction from other workflows had not been formalized. The task also asked whether input-matrix and permanent-water aggregation preserve multiple-area processing.

## Human input

The maintainer explained that external allocation records have already been placed and reviewed on a high-resolution MERIT Hydro river network. CaMa-Flood's second stage maps those records to its own unit catchments, and its river network is derived from MERIT Hydro. This is expected to make the network/catchment correspondence internally consistent, without proving every individual allocation. The maintainer identified the distributed global 1-minute and Japan 15-second layers as the intended CaMa-Flood allocation inputs. These are distinct from the AllocRiverGauge manual's first-stage global 30-second and Japan 5-second allocation-only maps; see the later clarification in `HI-0011` and `DR-0040`.

The maintainer further clarified that multi-area allocation is not a required future task for the initial v5 migration. Input-matrix and permanent-water tools have a separate multi-area aggregation role. The availability of the distributed Japan 15-second area is verified by package inspection; package-scale multi-area behavior remains unverified.

## Resulting interpretation

The v5 flow-gauge, dam, and level-gauge allocators retain the intentional one-area contract. Do not infer that the 1-minute grid is universally adequate for every basin. Do not classify multi-area allocation as an unresolved defect. Preserve the source-level multi-area behavior of input-matrix, regionalization, and permanent-water workflows.

## Evidence and formalization

Repository and package inspection independently verified the allocation reader's single-area rejection, one global 1-minute area and one Japan 15-second package area, and the loops over multiple areas in aggregation programs. The upstream v1.2 manual separately documents first-stage 30/5 arcsec maps. The technical specification is `docs/map_data_tools_plan.md`; the allocation workflow reference is `docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md`; the evidence distinction and validation boundary are in `DR-0039`, `DR-0040`, and `KG-0014`.

This clarification does not authorize algorithm changes, multi-area allocation implementation, or claims of large-package validation.
