# HI-0009 — Maintainer clarifies the Mekong additions to the GRanD list

- Intervention ID: `HI-0009`
- Linked event: `EV-0190`
- Task: `DT-0026`

## Trigger

The relationship of `GRanD_alloc+Mekong.csv` to the standard GRanD allocation list remained unclear after the tracking decision.

## Maintainer clarification

The maintainer explained that the file contains the GRanD list plus several recently constructed Mekong dams added by Yamazaki Laboratory following additional research. The maintainer also confirmed that the file is appropriate to include in Git tracking.

## Effect and limits

The broad provenance and reason for the alternate list are now documented. The exact added rows, supporting sources, and research date were not supplied, so no record-level claims are made.
