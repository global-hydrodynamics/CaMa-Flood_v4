# HI-0011 — Clarification of allocation-only versus distributed high-resolution maps

- Intervention ID: `HI-0011`
- Linked event ID(s): `EV-0204`
- Task ID: `DT-0029`
- Category: correction / map data contract
- Maintainer review: pending

## Situation and initial agent direction

Earlier documentation noted the AllocRiverGauge manual's global 30-arcsec and Japan 5-arcsec maps but could not establish how these related to the v4.30 package's global 1-minute and Japan 15-second map directories.

## Human input

The maintainer clarified that the global 30-second and Japan 5-second maps are for the AllocRiverGauge allocation workflow, while the CaMa-Flood distribution includes global 1-minute and Japan 15-second map layers, each as a single tile. The maintainer also confirmed that `dam_param.csv` belongs to a later `src_dam` workflow and was intentionally removed from the current registry review.

## Resulting change in direction

Treat the map sets as inputs to distinct workflow stages. Use the package's 1-minute global and 15-second Japan layers as CaMa-Flood map inputs; do not present the manual's 30/5-arcsec grids as the distributed CaMa-Flood layers. Keep `dam_param.csv` out of the allocation-stage registry scope.

## Implementation and validation evidence

The read-only v4.30 package inspection verified one global 1-minute `location.txt` area and one Japan 15-second area, with the latter declaring 6000 x 5280 cells and containing its binary layers and CTLs. The manual independently documents its 30/5-arcsec allocation-specific grids. The distinction is recorded in `docs/map_data_tools_plan.md`, `map/tools/script_params/README.md`, `KG-0014`, and `DR-0040`. Research schema and documentation checks are recorded in `VR-0067`.

## Significance and limitation

This clarification prevents confusing allocation's first-stage river-network maps with high-resolution map layers distributed alongside CaMa-Flood maps. Package metadata verifies availability and single-area layout, not scientific accuracy of any allocation record.
