# HI-0005 — Maintainer clarification of the permanent-water elevation filter

- Event: EV-0175
- Task: DT-0021 (document the `prmwat` elevation-filter rationale, Issue #122)
- Category: Scientific rationale and threshold-boundary clarification
- Review status: Pending maintainer review

The maintainer explained that `prmwat` represents permanent water within inundation CaMa-Flood can reproduce. Isolated permanent lakes high on hills lie outside the simulation's expected flood extent and must not be counted as pre-existing simulated water when post-processing newly inundated area. The maintainer described the intended elevation condition as `flddif < 5 m`.

The inspected legacy v4 and migrated v5 code use `flddif <= 5 m`. The follow-up updated explanatory comments and documentation but did not change the comparator or numerical behavior. The exact-5 m boundary remains a question for the maintainer. No private workstation path or transcript was copied; no stable archive identifier was provided.
