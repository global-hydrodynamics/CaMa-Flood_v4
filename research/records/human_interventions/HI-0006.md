## Situation and initial agent direction

The Phase A plan treated the GRDC and GRanD processed tables' redistribution status as unresolved and lacked a clear explanation of how those inputs relate to CaMa-Flood allocation. It proposed keeping real records external pending permission.

## Human input

The maintainer confirmed that the named processed GRDC and GRanD CSVs contain metadata only and redistribution is permitted. The maintainer explained that their first-stage MERIT Hydro allocation addressed reported drainage-area errors and discrepancies between MERIT Hydro and real-world drainage areas. Since CaMa-Flood networks are derived from MERIT Hydro, consistency with CaMa-Flood unit catchments is expected, but this does not establish every individual allocation's accuracy.

## Resulting change in direction at the time

The original response proposed tracking the complete two processed CSVs and distinguished maintainer context from the AllocRiverGauge manual. Later maintainer decisions confirmed full list tracking, including MLIT and the Mekong-added GRanD list; those subsequent directions are recorded separately. The basic two-stage method was already documented in the manual, so its current research classification is knowledge consolidation/accessibility improvement rather than new scientific recovery.

## Implementation and validation evidence

The technical summary is recorded in `research/records/discussions/DR-0034.md`, knowledge gap `KG-0016`, and `docs/map_allocation_migration_plan.md`. The manual review included the tool repository's v1.2 manual and the earlier v1.1 package copy. At the time of this intervention, no data, code, or ignore rules were changed. The later tool migration and its validation are recorded under Phase B. The manual and classification history are cross-referenced in `KG-0016` and `DR-0034`.

## Significance and limitation

This clarification avoids conflating redistribution of specifically approved metadata tables with source-database permissions and identifies the intended scientific boundary between high-resolution preparation and CaMa-Flood mapping. It is not independent scientific validation of station or reservoir locations.
